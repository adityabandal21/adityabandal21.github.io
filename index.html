RAG Document Q&A Chatbot

A retrieval-augmented chatbot that answers questions grounded in uploaded documents. Text is chunked and embedded, relevant passages are retrieved with FAISS, and answers are generated by Llama 3.3 70B served on Groq for low-latency inference.

LangChain FAISS Groq · Llama 3.3 70B Streamlit
  1. Chunk
  2. Embed
  3. Retrieve (FAISS)
  4. Generate (Llama 3.3)

Key feature: BERTScore faithfulness scoring on every answer

Customer Churn Predictor

An XGBoost classifier that predicts which customers are likely to churn, reaching 0.84 AUC. SHAP value attributions explain each prediction, turning the model into a tool a business can actually act on rather than a black box.

XGBoost SHAP 0.84 AUC

  2. XGBoost
  3. SHAP explanations

Key result: 0.84 AUC, with a reason behind every prediction
